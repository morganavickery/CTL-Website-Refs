Description

The Design-Based Research (DBR) Intern will work with the Associate Director of the Center for Teaching and Learning to conceptualize, design, implement, iterate on, and evaluate resources and programming related to the instructional design of courses taught at SUNY Cortland.   

Responsibilities may include:

  1. Participate in training related to design-based research methodology, instructional design, and evaluation of educational resources and programming.
  2. Work with the Associate Director to identify instructional design needs among SUNY Cortland faculty through existing data, consultations, or targeted inquiry.
  3. Conceptualize and design resources or programming (e.g., workshops, guides, templates, tools) responsive to identified faculty needs.
  4. Implement designed resources or programming in collaboration with CTL staff, and gather data on their use and impact.
  5. Iterate on designs based on feedback and evaluation data, applying DBR cycles of design, implementation, analysis, and redesign.
  6. Evaluate the effectiveness of designed resources or programming using appropriate qualitative and/or quantitative methods.

You (optionally) can contribute to writing focused on the design process, findings, or outcomes for potential publication or presentation.

By the end of the internship, you’ll be able to apply design-based research methodology to real instructional design problems, conceptualize and prototype educational resources responsive to faculty needs, evaluate the effectiveness and usability of designed interventions, and iterate on designs using evidence-based revision. If you pursue the writing option, you’ll also be able to document and communicate a design process in scholarly form.

Description

As part of SUNY Cortland’s Online Readiness Program, the Online Education Intern will work with the Senior Director of the Center for Teaching and Learning to design student-facing resources that support success in online courses. This internship is ideal for a student interested in education, communication, instructional design, digital media, accessibility, or online learning. 

The intern will receive training in basic instructional design principles, online education best practices, and student-centered resource development. They will help the CTL better understand what students need to feel prepared for online learning and will use that research to create practical resources for the campus community. 

Responsibilities may include:

  1. Participate in training related to instructional design, online education, student success, and accessibility.
  2. Gather student perspectives through surveys, interviews, or informal feedback about what helps students succeed in online courses.
  3. Review online education resources for students from other SUNY institutions and identify models that could inform SUNY Cortland’s approach.
  4. Work with the Senior Director to develop student-facing materials for the Online Learning website, such as short videos, web content, checklists, guides, or frequently asked questions.
  5. Collaborate with CTL staff and other interns to share and promote completed resources.

By the end of the internship, the student will be able to conduct a basic needs assessment, evaluate educational resources for usability and relevance, apply instructional design principles to student-facing materials, and produce digital resources that support student success in online learning environments.
